Reference numbers are the same on every page. New references are added to the end of the list so existing numbers never change.

Image credits

  • All EEG traces, spectra and spectrograms are original synthetic figures made for this site with tools/make_figures.py. They are licensed with the rest of the site (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Their layouts are modelled on figures in Purdon et al. 2015[1], which are linked from the text and not reproduced, because the publisher holds copyright and some of those figures are themselves reproduced from other sources.
  • The haemodynamic decision table in Module 5 is adapted from Lee et al. 2021, Fig. 2, under CC BY-NC 4.0[2]. Changes were made: it was redrawn as a table, cells not described in the source text were extrapolated, and the wording was changed.
  • The learning outcomes on the curriculum map are reproduced verbatim from Berger-Estilita et al. 2026[3].
  • The ShARP artefact mnemonic and the course structure are from the Safe Brain Initiative boot camp, CC BY 4.0[4].
