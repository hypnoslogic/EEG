Running a local session

The boot camp authors offer to share their teaching resources free of charge and to help set up boot camps run by local experts[4]. Hands-on stations work well: applying electrodes and operating monitors, quizzes on signals, artefacts and drug signatures, and video cases[4]. The companion script in simulation mode can serve as a live demonstration when no patient monitor is available.

For education only. This is not a clinical protocol and does not replace your monitor's instructions for use, your department's guidelines or clinical judgement. Every EEG image on this site is synthetic unless stated otherwise.
