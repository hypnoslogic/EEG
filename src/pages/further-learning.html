Running a local session

The boot camp authors offer to share their teaching resources free of charge and to help set up boot camps run by local experts{{cite:sbi2025}}. Hands-on stations work well: applying electrodes and operating monitors, quizzes on signals, artefacts and drug signatures, and video cases{{cite:sbi2025}}. The companion script in simulation mode can serve as a live demonstration when no patient monitor is available.
