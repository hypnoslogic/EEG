Planned features

  • Interactive "build a spectrum" demo. Mix slow, delta, theta, alpha and beta with sliders and watch the raw trace, spectrum and DSA change. The site is static for now.
  • Quizzes and flashcards for drug signatures and artefacts ({{lo:CI5}}), following the boot camp's quiz rounds{{cite:sbi2025}}.
  • ICU sedation section, using the international expert consensus on pEEG-guided sedation in critically ill adults{{cite:rasulo2023}}. This mirrors the boot camp's "transfer from theatre to ICU" block{{cite:sbi2025}}.
  • Real clinical cases ({{lo:CI1}} {{lo:CI2}}): de-identified EEG recordings, for example logged with the companion script, with consent and ethics approval, shown alongside annotated monitor screenshots.
  • Paediatric module: age-dependent EEG patterns and the emergence delirium evidence{{cite:haidar2026}}.
  • Advanced topics: epileptiform activity ({{lo:AC7}}), multitaper spectra, burst suppression probability.
  • Update mechanism ({{lo:PL4}}): a six-monthly evidence search, and an "evidence watch" list of registered trials.
